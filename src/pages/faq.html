How it works

Does it work with the fridge I already have?

Yes. Fridge Bot is an add-on camera, so it doesn't need a smart fridge. If your fridge has shelves and Wi-Fi reaches the kitchen, it should work.

How does it know when food is going bad?

It recognizes each item, records the day it first appeared, and compares that to typical shelf life for that food. For packaged items it can also read printed dates when the label faces the camera. You can correct any guess in the app and it learns from that.

What about things hidden behind other things?

Items the camera can't see stay on your list from earlier photos until they're gone, so a jar pushed to the back doesn't vanish. The app marks those as "last seen" with a date.

Do I have to scan barcodes or type in groceries?

No. That's the point. You put food away like normal and close the door; the list updates from the photo.

What if it gets an item wrong?

Tap the item in the app to fix its name, count or date. Corrections improve future guesses.

Hardware

Won't the lens fog up in the cold?

The lens has an anti-fog coating, and the camera is designed for 0 to 8°C (32 to 46°F) with high humidity.

How is it powered?

A rechargeable battery. Because it only wakes up to take one photo per door close, a charge is designed to last months.

What Wi-Fi does it need?

2.4 GHz Wi-Fi that reaches your kitchen. Most home routers broadcast this.

Do I need tools to install it?

No. It clips onto the top shelf or sticks on with an adhesive pad. No drilling or wiring.

Privacy

Does it record video?

No. It takes single still photos, only when the door closes.

Could it ever see people?

It faces into the fridge and only takes a photo after the door has shut, using its own small light, so people are never in frame.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are processed only to recognize food, and you can delete your history at any time. Your fridge contents are never sold to advertisers. See our privacy promise.
