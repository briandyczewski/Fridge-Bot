How it works

Set it up once. Then just open your fridge like normal.

See the full walkthrough
01

Stick the camera to the top shelf

The camera clips or sticks inside the fridge and faces the shelves. It's wide-angle and anti-fog, so it can see the whole fridge through the cold.

02

It takes a photo when the door closes

A light sensor knows when the door shuts. The camera takes one photo and sends it over Wi-Fi. No live video, and nothing outside the fridge.

03

AI updates your list on your phone

A vision model picks out each item, keeps track of when it first showed up, and estimates how long it has left. Your phone gets the list.
