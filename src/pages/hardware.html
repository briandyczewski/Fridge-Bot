Home/Hardware

Built for 3°C and a door that opens forty times a day.

A small, battery-powered camera that lives on your top shelf. No wiring, no tools, and nothing outside the fridge.

Specifications

Small camera, cold-proof.

Camera
Wide-angle lens with an anti-fog coating
Trigger
Light sensor, one photo per door close
Operating range
0 to 8°C (32 to 46°F), high humidity
Power
Rechargeable battery, months per charge
Connection
2.4 GHz Wi-Fi
Mounting
Clip or adhesive, no tools or wiring

These are design targets for the first version. Final specs will be confirmed after prototype testing.

Privacy

It only sees your groceries.

  • The camera faces into the fridge and only takes a photo after the door has shut, using its own small light, so people are never in frame.
  • Photos are processed only to recognize food, and you can delete your history at any time.
  • Your fridge contents are never sold to advertisers.

Compatibility

Fridge Bot is an add-on camera, so it doesn't need a smart fridge. If your fridge has shelves and Wi-Fi reaches the kitchen, it should work.
