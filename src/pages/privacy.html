Fridge Bot is in development. This page describes our privacy commitments for the product. A full legal privacy policy will be published before any device ships.

What the camera captures

  • Still photos only. No live video and no audio.
  • Only after the door shuts. A light sensor triggers one photo per door close, using the camera's own small light.
  • Facing in. The camera points into the fridge, so people are never in frame.

What happens to photos

Photos are processed only to recognize food: naming items, counting them, and reading printed dates when a label faces the camera. The results become your fridge list in the app.

Your control

  • You can delete your photo and item history at any time from the app.
  • You can correct any item, and those corrections stay tied to your account.
  • Taking the camera out of the fridge stops all capture immediately.

What we'll never do

  • Your fridge contents are never sold to advertisers.
  • We won't use the camera for anything other than recognizing food.

Waitlist data

If you join the waitlist, we use your email to tell you about beta access and launch news. Optional details like your fridge type help us choose beta testers. You can unsubscribe from any email.
