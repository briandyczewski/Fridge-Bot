Home/The app

Your fridge, in your pocket.

At work, at the store, or on the couch: open Fridge Bot and see what's in there right now, what to eat first, and what you could make tonight.

Interactive demo

What needs eating, first.

The home screen sorts everything by days left. Red means today, amber means soon. That's usually the whole decision about dinner.

Everything, shelf by shelf.

Filter by shelf to check the crisper from the dairy aisle. Items hidden behind others stay listed with a "last seen" date.

Meals from what's there.

Recipe ideas built around what needs using, so dinner clears the shelf instead of adding to it.

See what you saved.

A monthly tally of food eaten in time versus thrown out, in pounds and in dollars.

Features

Small nudges, at the right moment.

Use-first reminders

A short nudge when something is about to turn, sent in time to actually cook it.

Check from the store

Standing in the dairy aisle? Look at the latest photo and skip the second carton.

Meals from what's there

Recipe ideas built around the items that need using, so dinner clears the shelf.
