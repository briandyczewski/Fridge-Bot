Home/About

Less forgotten food. That's the whole idea.

Most food doesn't get thrown out because it's bad. It gets thrown out because it was forgotten. Fridge Bot exists to make the fridge remember for you.

Why we started

Smart fridges solved this. For $3,700.

Leftovers get pushed behind new groceries. People buy what's already at home. Nobody remembers how long the chili has been in there. The average U.S. family of four throws away about $2,900 of food a year, and 77% of Americans say they waste food because they forget it's in the fridge.

Cameras that recognize food already exist, but only inside fridges that cost thousands, and most people keep a fridge 10 to 15 years. The cheaper add-on cameras still make you type in what you bought.

So we're building the missing piece: the smart fridge, without buying a new fridge. A small camera, a good vision model, and an app that tells you what to eat first.

What we believe

Three rules we design by.

01 · Zero effort

If you have to type it, you won't.

No barcode scanning, no logging groceries. The fridge gets tracked because you opened and closed it, nothing more.

02 · Privacy first

It only sees your groceries.

Photos are taken facing in, after the door shuts. Your fridge contents are never sold to advertisers.

03 · Keep your fridge

The best fridge is the one you own.

Replacing a working appliance to get a feature is its own kind of waste. Fridge Bot is an add-on.
